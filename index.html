Business Question

Which customer segments drive revenue, and where is revenue at risk?

Approach

  • Data Cleaning: Removed cancelled orders, returns, non-positive quantities and prices — 1,067,371 raw rows → 779,425 clean rows.
  • RFM Segmentation: Scored each customer 1–5 on Recency, Frequency and Monetary value.
  • Cohort Retention: Grouped customers by first-purchase month and tracked month-over-month retention.
  • Power BI Dashboard: Built 5 visuals including a cohort retention heatmap.

Key Findings

Champions drive 68% of revenue

Despite being only 22% of customers.

£1.09M in At-Risk revenue

353 high-value customers showing declining engagement.

Retention declining in newer cohorts

Month-1 retention fell from 35% (2009) to 17% (2010).
